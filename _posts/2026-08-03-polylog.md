<h6><b>Abstract</b></h6>
<p> Dawson-Nielsen (2005) presented a poly-log diameter bound \(\mathcal{O}(\log^{\alpha}(1/\epsilon))\) on \(\text{SU}(2)\) and \(\text{SU}(d)\) with exponent \(\alpha<4\) arbitrarily close. Dinai (2006) extended this result to the family of finite groups \(\text{SL}_{2}(\mathbb{Z}/p^{n}\mathbb{Z})\) with identical exponent. In this work, we introduce \(p\)-adic analogues of techniques on Elkasapy words from Kuperberg (2025) to $\(\text{SL}_{2}(\mathbb{Z}_{p})\), improving the poly-log diameter bound from Dinai to an exponent \(\alpha > \log_{\phi}(2) \approx 1.44042\ldots\) arbitrarily close. </p>
